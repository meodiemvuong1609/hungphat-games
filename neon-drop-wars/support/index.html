Neon Drop Wars – Support

Need help or found a bug? Email us at contact@hungphatelevator.com. Please include your device model, OS version and app version (shown in your store's app page).

FAQ

How do I play? Pick a team, then tap above the board to drop balls. Score more points than the three rival teams before the 60-second timer ends to win and level up.

Does the game need internet? No. It plays fully offline.

Are there ads or purchases? No ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I reset my progress? Uninstall and reinstall the app (Android: or clear the app's storage in Settings).

Privacy: see our Privacy Policy. We collect no personal data.
