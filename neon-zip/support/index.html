Neon Zip – Support

Need help or found a bug? Email us at contact@hungphatelevator.com. Please include your device model, OS version, app version (shown in your store's app page) and the level number if relevant.

FAQ

How do I play? Tap an arrow to send it flying off the board in the direction it points. It only flies if its path is clear; tapping a blocked arrow costs one of your 3 hearts. Clear every arrow to win the level.

How are stars awarded? 3 stars for no mistakes, 2 stars for one mistake, 1 star for two mistakes.

I'm stuck on a level. Every board can always be solved. Tap HINT to highlight a safe arrow. You earn an extra hint (up to 9) the first time you get 3 stars on a level.

How does the Daily Puzzle work? There is a new board each day based on your device's date. Clear it on consecutive days to grow your streak.

How do I unlock themes? Collect stars: Synth at 30, Ice at 90, Lava at 180 and Gold at 300 stars.

Does the game need internet? No. It plays fully offline.

Are there ads or purchases? No ads and no in-app purchases.

Will I lose my progress if I update the app? No. Progress is stored on your device and kept across updates. Uninstalling the app (or, on Android, clearing its storage) resets it.
