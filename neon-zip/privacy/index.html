Neon Zip – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Neon Zip (the "App"), published by Hung Phat Elevator ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the App on Apple iOS/iPadOS and Google Android.

Summary: Neon Zip does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. The App works completely offline.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any information from you. Specifically, the App:

  • does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account;
  • does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or other device data;
  • does not use advertising, advertising identifiers, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs;
  • does not connect to the internet (on Android it does not even request the network permission);
  • does not contain in-app purchases.

2. Information stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the App saves a few game settings locally on your device using the operating system's standard storage (UserDefaults on iOS, SharedPreferences/DataStore on Android):

  • highest unlocked level and the stars earned on each level;
  • number of hints available;
  • Daily Puzzle streak, best streak and the date of the last Daily Puzzle you cleared;
  • selected colour theme and whether the tutorial has been shown;
  • sound and haptics on/off settings.

The Daily Puzzle is chosen from your device's local date; the date is used only on your device and is never sent anywhere.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. It may be included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud or Google backup) according to your device settings, which are governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policies. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App (or, on Android, by clearing the App's storage).

3. Sharing of information

Because we do not collect any information, we do not share or sell information with any third party.

4. Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Since the App collects no personal information at all, no personal information from children is collected, used or disclosed. Parents or guardians with questions may contact us using the details below.

5. Security

No personal data is transmitted or stored by us. Game progress is kept in the App's private storage on your device, protected by your device's operating system.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or Vietnam's personal data protection law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete; the locally stored game progress is fully under your control as described in section 2.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes how information is handled (for example by adding online features), we will update this policy and the "Effective date" above before that version is released.
